\begin{abstract}
Analog peripherals with continuous depth-sensing hardware offer sub-millimetre
actuation precision, yet their firmware often relies on static thresholds that
ignore per-user biomechanical variance and degrade under neuromotor fatigue.
This thesis addresses that gap by designing an end-to-end click-sensitivity
recommendation pipeline for Haptic Inductive Trigger System (HITS) devices,
evaluated on a corpus of 132 annotated recordings from 39 participants
($\approx$3.4 hours of 1\,kHz telemetry).

The system makes three contributions.
(1)~A supervised labeling workflow that bootstraps frame-level annotations from
algorithmic criteria and iteratively improves them through model-assisted
refinement; the heuristic alone achieves a deployment F1 of only 0.373,
confirming that human review is essential for the firmware-unregistered and
noise-confounded cases that are most informative for model training.
(2)~A four-class temporal intent-detection model supporting three architectures:
a Random Forest that achieves a deployment F1 of 0.967 on the left channel
(LOPO mean $0.921 \pm 0.024$) with make- and break-boundary bias under
1.5\,ms, a Gradient Boosted Trees variant with asymmetric soft-label targets
encoding the directional cost asymmetry of early versus late onset detection,
and a dilated residual FCN with a 3\,060-frame receptive field whose
cross-participant generalisation collapses to $0.597 \pm 0.174$ on the
data-scarce right-button corpus, motivating larger collection campaigns.
A probe ablation reveals that 10 raw depth samples at $\pm$10\,ms offsets
contribute $+2.17\%$ deployment F1 --- three times the combined gain of all
explicit kinematic derivative channels ($+0.68\%$), indicating that probe
range and density are the primary levers for RF improvement.
(3)~A firmware-faithful threshold optimizer that exhaustively evaluates the
discrete (AP, RT) grid via a Rapid Trigger FSM simulator. Across 238
channel-records the optimizer reduces mean make latency from 31.2\,ms to
14.4\,ms~--- a 16.8\,ms improvement spanning $\approx$1.5 perceptual
just-noticeable differences~--- and increases the true-positive detection
rate from 90.1\,\% to 95.0\,\% (Wilcoxon signed-rank $W=0$, $n=238$,
$p\ll 0.001$), confirming that no session benefits from the factory default.
\end{abstract}
